PREFATORY NOTE.

THESE papers are specially designed to supply lessons, written or _vivâ voce_, for boys who have pretty well mastered the Public School Latin Primer, or Allen's Latin Grammar, but have yet to make sure of their ground before they can use more advanced grammars as books of reference. Most of the papers, it is thought, may be done, and revised _vivâ voce_, in three quarters of an hour. They may be shortened in most instances by omitting specified questions or parts of questions. The harder ones are enclosed between square brackets [...], the easier between asterisks *  •. No practical teacher will object to certain questions being repeated.

J. H. R

ST. MARY'S, BECCLES,
