In this folder are the original PDF scans of the two textbooks.

PDFs of Nation Latin Exams can be downloaded from the [NLE website](https://www.nle.org/previous-exams-and-answer-keys). We use all exams from the years 2015, 2020, and 2025.

PDFs of Certamen questions can be downloaded from the Question Archive on the [Certamen website](https://www.njcl.org/NJCL-Convention/Convention-Contests/Certamen). We use the [1996-2009 questions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1F9AMt7Hy7BqDJ23Q7_Lwy2YKfDWc4NwG/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=106574327601452428399&rtpof=true&sd=true).
